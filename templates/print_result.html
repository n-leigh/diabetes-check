Health screening tool — not a medical diagnosis. This summary is generated for educational and preliminary screening purposes. It estimates complication risk using statistical models trained on population health data (CDC NHANES and BRFSS clinical cohorts) and evidence-aligned clinical rule scoring. The rule-based score and model estimate use different methods and should not be compared directly. These results support screening and education, not diagnosis. A lower estimate does not rule out disease, and a higher estimate does not confirm disease. Please share this summary with a licensed healthcare provider for interpretation and any decisions about your care.
