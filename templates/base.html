Intended Use & Clinical Disclaimer

DiaBeates is a preliminary screening and risk projection tool designed for educational and informational use. It is not a diagnostic device.

The results provided do not constitute medical advice or a formal clinical diagnosis. They should not be used alone to make decisions about treatment or care.

Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for medical evaluation, interpretation of your results, and guidance about your health.
