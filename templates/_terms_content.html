1. What DiaBeates does

DiaBeates asks 15 questions, plus optional lab values, and estimates your result for four areas: heart, kidneys, nerves and feet, and eyes. Results come from a transparent set of rules and statistical models. They are estimates to help you prepare questions for your doctor.

2. Not medical advice

DiaBeates is not a medical device. It does not give a diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. A result of "Good" does not mean you are healthy, and a result of "Check" or "See a doctor" does not mean you have a condition. If you feel unwell or think you may have an emergency, contact your doctor or your local emergency number (911 in the Philippines) right away. Please do not delay care because of a result here.

3. Who can use it

DiaBeates is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it together with a parent or guardian.
