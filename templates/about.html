About DiaBeates

A calm, simple health check made for families in the Philippines.

What this is

DiaBeates is a free health screening website made for ordinary people and families in the Philippines. It helps you check whether high blood sugar might be affecting your heart, kidneys, feet, or eyes before serious problems start. DiaBeates is a screening aid and is not a diagnostic device.

How we work out your results

We compare your answers with proven medical guidelines from diabetes specialists. We check whether your blood pressure, sugar, weight, and daily habits show signs of strain. Then we group your risk into three clear levels: Good, Worth a check, or See a doctor soon.

Your privacy

We do not ask for your name, phone number, or email address. We never track you or sell your health answers. Everything you type stays private on your phone or computer.

For doctors and researchers: clinical documentation & citations

Dual-Engine Clinical Architecture

DiaBeates executes a dual-tier screening pipeline combining deterministic rule-matrix scoring with calibrated machine-learning classification models.

Guideline Alignments & Cohorts

  • Cardiovascular: 2019 ACC/AHA Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease Guideline. Logistic Regression trained on CDC NHANES (N=2,488, AUROC 0.7716, Brier 0.1691).
  • Kidneys (Nephropathy): KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease. Evaluated against stage-stratified albuminuria/eGFR risk matrices.
  • Nerves & Mobility: Michigan Neuropathy Screening Instrument (MNSI) physical impairment criteria. Gradient Boosting Classifier trained on CDC BRFSS (N=5,000, AUROC 0.7992, Brier 0.1765).
  • Retinopathy: ADA Standards of Care Retinopathy screening protocol. Logistic Regression trained on CDC NHANES (AUROC 0.6420, Brier 0.1636). Given lower discrimination in survey data, annual dilated eye exams are advised universally.
