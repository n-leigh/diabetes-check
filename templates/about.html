DiaBeates is a web-based diabetes risk screening aid that helps individuals estimate their likelihood of developing diabetes-related complications based on their health indicators.

What DiaBeates does

Diabetes can progress quietly, and many complications become more difficult to manage when they are identified too late. DiaBeates is designed to make that first step easier by translating routine health information into a practical risk summary.

DiaBeates was built to assess risk in two ways at once: a transparent, clinically-led rule matrix and a trained classification model, shown side by side to support earlier follow-up and better-informed discussion with clinicians.

Early screening matters because small changes in blood pressure, cholesterol, lifestyle, mobility, and overall health burden can signal elevated risk before significant illness develops. DiaBeates supports informed decision-making by turning those signals into a clear, explainable summary that is easy to understand and discuss with a healthcare professional.

Transparent Rule Logic

Structured clinical rules

Encodes established risk factors (hypertension, cholesterol, smoking, mobility difficulty, general health burden) into a transparent point system, tied to ADA Standards of Care and NHANES threshold research. Every score is explainable, with no black box.

Predictive Modeling

Statistical classification

In addition to rule-based assessment, the system applies statistical classification models to detect patterns in the submitted health indicators and generate a category prediction for comparison with the rule-matrix result.

Built for clarity, transparency, and action

Every assessment is checked for required inputs and valid values before analysis so the screening estimate stays complete, consistent, and clinically meaningful.

Ethics, Limitations, and Disclaimer

⚠ Not a diagnostic tool

DiaBeates is a health screening aid built to demonstrate classification modeling on a healthcare screening problem. It is not a certified clinical validation medical device. Risk levels show relative estimates, not diagnoses. Results should never replace evaluation by a licensed healthcare provider, and no treatment decision should be made from this tool's output alone.

Empirical Ground Truth from CDC Clinical Registries

In DiaBeates (Version 2.0-clinical), machine learning models are trained directly on authentic clinical endpoints from CDC National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) cohorts (physician-diagnosed CVD, kidney laboratory-confirmed CKD, and digital retinal photography grading) and the CDC BRFSS registry (functional mobility impairment). The clinical rule matrix operates as an independent, transparent guideline explanation layer and does not provide training labels, ensuring completely non-circular, empirical risk estimation.

Dataset Scope & Clinical Targets

Training data is curated from CDC NHANES (2017–2021, 2022–2023, 2007–2008) and CDC BRFSS public-section cohorts. The optional point-of-care laboratory layer evaluates HbA1c against ADA 2026 Standards of Care (<7.0% optimal), Systolic Blood Pressure against KDIGO 2024 / ADA targets (<120 mmHg optimal), and LDL cholesterol against ADA 2026 primary prevention targets (<70 mg/dL optimal for diabetes).

Data handling

Data Processing & Local Storage

You can use DiaBeates without an account. Guest checks stay for 90 days. If you create an account, we store your email, nickname, and saved checks until you delete the account. Accounts have user and admin roles. Admins see account details and safe totals only, never individual health results or inputs. Data is processed by this local Flask application and is not sent to cloud services, remote APIs, third-party analytics, or data fonts. You may view, export, correct, or delete your data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
