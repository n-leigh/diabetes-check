Health Screening

Understand your risk
before complications advance

DiaBeates is a web-based diabetes risk screening tool that helps individuals estimate their likelihood of developing diabetes-related complications over the next 5 and 10 years.

What DiaBeates does

Diabetes can progress quietly, and many complications become more difficult to manage when they are identified too late. DiaBeates is designed to make that first step easier by translating routine health information into a practical risk assessment.

DiaBeates was built to assess risk in two ways at once: a transparent, clinically-cited rule matrix and a trained classification model, shown side by side to support earlier follow-up and better-informed discussion with clinicians.

Early screening matters because small changes in blood pressure, cholesterol, lifestyle, mobility, and overall health burden can signal elevated risk before significant illness develops. DiaBeates supports informed decision-making by turning those signals into a clear, explainable summary that is easy to understand and discuss with a healthcare professional.

Transparent Risk Logic

Structured clinical rules

Encodes established risk factors (hypertension, cholesterol, smoking, mobility difficulty, general health burden) into a transparent point system, cited to ADA Standards of Care and NHANES threshold research. Every score is explainable, no black box.

Predictive Modeling

Statistical classification

In addition to rule-based assessment, the system applies statistical classification models to detect patterns in patient data and generate a risk projection aligned with established clinical indicators.

Built for clarity, accuracy, and action

01

Complete-data validation

Every assessment is checked for complete clinical inputs before analysis so that risk projections are based on accurate, usable information.

02

Personalized guidance

Results are paired with practical health guidance designed to help users understand what the risk level may mean and which habits or follow-up steps deserve attention.

Referral advisories

For higher-risk cases, the system can suggest when a physician review may be appropriate, helping users act sooner when elevated risk warrants medical attention.

Ethics, Limitations & Disclaimer

Not a diagnostic tool

DiaBeates is an academic case study built to demonstrate classification modeling on a healthcare screening problem, it is not a certified or clinically validated medical device. Risk levels shown are statistical estimates, not diagnoses. Results should never replace evaluation by a licensed healthcare provider, and no treatment decision should be made on this tool's output alone.

Ground truth comes from rules, not diagnosed outcomes

The classifiers were trained on labels generated by our own rule matrix, not on confirmed clinical diagnoses of complications. This means the model's job is to learn to approximate the rule matrix efficiently, not to discover new medical patterns. Two of the three categories reach near-perfect accuracy for exactly this reason: their underlying rules are simple enough that a classifier can reconstruct them almost exactly. This is expected and disclosed here rather than presented as a stronger result than it is.

Dataset scope

Training data comes from the CDC's BRFSS survey, self-reported health data from U.S. respondents, not lab-confirmed clinical records. Findings may not generalize to populations, healthcare systems, or reporting norms outside that context. The optional lab-values layer (HbA1c, blood pressure, LDL) is scored against cited clinical thresholds, but has not itself been clinically validated as a predictive tool.

Data handling

Assessments entered into this system are stored locally in the app's own database for demonstration purposes (e.g. the History page). No real patient data, consent process, or production-grade privacy safeguards are involved, this system is not intended for deployment with real patient records without further ethical review and safeguards.
