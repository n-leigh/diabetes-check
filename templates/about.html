DiaBeates is a web-based diabetes risk screening tool that helps individuals estimate their likelihood of developing diabetes-related complications based on their health indicators.

What DiaBeates does

Diabetes can progress quietly, and major microvascular and macrovascular complications become significantly harder to manage when identified late. DiaBeates translates routine patient indicators and optional point-of-care laboratory findings into actionable risk assessments.

DiaBeates employs a Dual-Engine Clinical Architecture: an evidence-grounded Clinical Rule Matrix (Version 2.0-clinical) running concurrently alongside calibrated machine learning models trained on authentic CDC clinical cohorts, presented side by side for transparent evaluation.

Early risk identification matters because incremental elevations in blood pressure, atherogenic lipids, glycemic load, and mobility friction signal progressive organ damage before irreversible complications occur.

Transparent Rule Logic

Clinical Rule Matrix (v2.0)

Grounds risk scoring directly in published clinical consensus: ACC/AHA 10-Year ASCVD & UKPDS for cardiovascular disease, KDIGO 2024 for diabetic nephropathy, and the Michigan Neuropathy Screening Instrument (MNSI) for peripheral neuropathy.

Predictive Modeling

Calibrated ML Classifiers

Trained on authentic CDC NHANES and BRFSS diabetic cohorts with diagnosed complications. Models output calibrated risk probabilities alongside discrimination metrics (AUROC) and calibration error (Brier Score).

Ethics, Limitations, and Clinical Scope

Preliminary screening tool — not a diagnostic device

DiaBeates is an educational clinical decision support platform designed to facilitate early risk awareness and informed patient-clinician discussion. It is not a certified medical device. Risk levels are statistical projections, not definitive diagnoses. Results should never replace formal clinical evaluation, and no treatment or prescription adjustments should be made based on this tool alone.

Authentic clinical cohorts & epidemiological training

Machine learning models are trained on authentic cohorts: the CDC NHANES 2017–2018 diabetic cohort with diagnosed cardiovascular disease (N=949), the CDC NHANES 2021–2023 cohort with laboratory-confirmed CKD staging (N=848), and the CDC BRFSS diabetic cohort for functional mobility and neuropathy indicators (N=35,346). Models undergo probability calibration (CalibratedClassifierCV) and are evaluated via discrimination (AUROC) and calibration reliability (Brier score).

Population generalizability

The underlying cohorts represent adult populations within the United States. Risk coefficients and baseline event rates may differ across international populations, varied genetic predispositions, and distinct healthcare delivery models.

Data security, privacy, and session isolation

Assessments are scoped to anonymous client sessions and stored locally using SQLite with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL). All submissions are protected by CSRF validation and application rate limits. Users have full control over their assessment history, with complete capabilities to archive, restore, and delete records.
